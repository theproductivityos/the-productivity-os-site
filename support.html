FAQ

Common questions.

Not finding your answer? Email directly.

Do I need Obsidian plugins?

The vault uses Templater for dynamic date fields. Everything else is standard markdown and works in Obsidian without a complicated setup.

Which AI tools does it support?

The prompts are tool-agnostic and written for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Paid plans are not required, but stronger models usually produce better output.

Is this Mac-only?

The vault runs anywhere Obsidian runs: Mac, Windows, Linux, iOS, and Android. The Raycast bonus is Mac-only because Raycast is a Mac app.

Can I use it with an existing vault?

Yes. You can use it as a standalone vault or drop the folder structure into your existing Obsidian setup.

What is included in v1.0?

13 vault folders, 80 templates, 70+ AI prompts, structured YAML frontmatter on every file, onboarding guides, and the Raycast Integration Bonus.

What about refunds?

Because this is an instant digital download, all sales are final. I've included detailed previews, folder breakdowns, and setup guidance so you can understand exactly what you're getting before you buy. If something is broken or missing from your download, reach out and I'll make it right.

Is this beginner-friendly?

Yes. The Quick Start Guide walks you through setup, and the vault is designed so you can start with one area, like Daily Brain or Projects, instead of using everything at once.

Is this just a folder of templates?

No. The templates share a consistent structure, naming system, frontmatter model, and cross-linking approach, so the vault works as one connected operating base.

Can I customize it?

Yes. Everything is markdown, so you can edit, duplicate, remove, or reorganize files as your system evolves.

What should I start with?

Start with Daily Brain, Projects, and AI Command Center. Those three areas give you the fastest path to daily use.
